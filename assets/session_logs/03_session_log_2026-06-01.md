## Lab Results

The FBI lab returned a one-paragraph report: the canal markings were made with a carbide-tipped hand tool, post-installation. Not random. No match to known gang symbols or graffiti tagging conventions. No further conclusions. The lab suggested consulting an anthropologist.

Brody had already made the call. Dr. Raymond Okafor at Arizona State University had been identified as the leading academic on Hohokam material culture in the Phoenix area. He agreed to meet the agents at his office in Tempe.

## Arizona State University — Okafor's Office

Okafor's office was dense with books and site photographs. He was cordial but grew visibly quieter as the agents spread the photos of the canal markings across his desk. He identified the symbols without hesitation as modified Hohokam petroglyphs — specifically a sub-category associated with water ceremonies and the control of seasonal flooding. The modifications, he said, were recent. Someone had added a repeating third element to each symbol that he didn't recognise from the archaeological record.

He mentioned, seemingly as an aside, a 1964 report by an anthropologist named Jessup: *Aquatic Mysticism Among Modern Hohokam Descendant Communities*. Jessup had documented a small group in the south Phoenix area who called themselves *Sihu* — O'odham for "flower" — and practised rituals centred on the canal network as a living spiritual system. The report had been quietly pulled from the university catalogue after one semester. Okafor said he didn't know why.

He did not look at the photos again after identifying the symbols. When Jeppe asked him directly whether he thought the disappearances were connected to the markings, he said he thought the agents should be careful near the canal at night.

## Earl Dutton

That evening, Phoenix PD dispatch put out a notification: an individual matching Dutton's description had been found wandering barefoot on W. Baseline Road, approximately half a kilometre from the canal site, at around 9 p.m. He was non-verbal and disoriented. He was transported to St. Joseph's Hospital.

Nicolai drove to St. Joseph's. Dutton was sedated by the time he arrived. A nurse said he had been repeating the same phrase when brought in: *they go down into the water*. Nicolai sat in the corridor for an hour, then drove back.

The Jessup report did not appear in any library catalogue they could find.
