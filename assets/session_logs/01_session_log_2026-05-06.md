## Briefing

Brody called Jeppe and Nicolai into his office at the Phoenix Field Office on a Monday morning. He kept it brief: three homeless individuals had disappeared from a restored section of the old Hohokam canal network along the south edge of the city over the past six weeks. Frank Tenorio (68), Angela Morales (55), and Dale "Bucky" Ostergard (44). Phoenix PD had written them up as runaways or overdoses and moved on. But then Earl Dutton — a long-term informant who worked the same area — failed to make his weekly check-in after leaving a voicemail saying he had "seen something wrong" near the canal. That made it federal.

Brody handed over a thin manila folder and told them not to embarrass the office.

## Phoenix PD — South Mountain Precinct

The agents drove to the South Mountain precinct and met Detective Margaret Vasquez, who had caught two of the three missing persons reports. She was polite but made no effort to conceal her opinion that the FBI was wasting everyone's time. She shared her files without redaction: no physical evidence of foul play, no witnesses, no pattern beyond proximity to the canal. She pointed out, not without satisfaction, that the canal area attracted a transient population, and transients sometimes just moved on.

Jeppe asked whether any of the three had known each other. Vasquez paused before answering — Tenorio and Morales had both used the same overnight shelter on W. Baseline Road. She hadn't considered that a significant connection. She agreed to pull shelter logs and send them over.

## Canal Site

The agents drove south to the section of restored canal where Dutton had last been reported. The canal here was a shallow concrete-lined channel, largely dry in April, running between scrubland and a chain-link fence bordering a storage yard. The afternoon light was flat and very bright.

Walking the bank, Nicolai noticed a series of marks scratched into the concrete lining of the channel — geometric, deliberate, repeating. Not graffiti. Too methodical, too small. He photographed them. Jeppe found a similar cluster fifty metres further along, partially obscured by blown sand.

As they returned to the car, Jeppe looked back across the canal road and saw a man standing very still on the opposite bank, watching them. He was wearing a pale shirt and appeared to be middle-aged. By the time Jeppe had pointed him out, the man was gone. There was nowhere obvious he could have gone quickly.

They drove back to the field office. The photos were sent to the lab.
