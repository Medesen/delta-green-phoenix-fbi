## St. Joseph's Hospital

Dutton was more coherent in the morning. He recognised Jeppe and gripped his forearm. He spoke slowly, in fragments, and would stop mid-sentence and stare at the wall for long intervals.

What the agents pieced together: Dutton had followed a group of four or five people along the canal after dark, thinking they might be connected to the disappearances. They had walked to a point further south than the agents had surveyed — beyond the restored section, where the old canal infrastructure was partially intact and unlit. He lost them. Then he was in water, cold water, and there was a sound like breathing that was not his own. He did not know how he got from there to Baseline Road.

He said: *the canal goes down*. When Nicolai pointed out that the canal was a concrete surface channel with no basement or underground section, Dutton looked at him with something close to pity.

He also mentioned a white building with a blue door. He had seen it before, from a bus. Somewhere off W. Southern Avenue.

## Property Records

Back at the field office, Jeppe pulled county property records for the corridor along W. Southern Avenue south of the canal. An irrigation infrastructure company called Southwestern Canal Services had operated a maintenance depot at a rural address off the avenue from 1948 until 1971, when it was sold to an unknown private buyer. The structure on file was a single-storey concrete block building.

A Google Maps satellite image of the address, printed in the field office, showed a white-rendered building set back from a dirt access road. The image was four years old and the resolution was poor. The door, if it was visible, was the right colour.

## Okafor — Follow-up

Jeppe called Okafor to ask where a physical copy of the Jessup report might be held if not in the catalogue. Okafor was silent for a moment, then said there had been a copy in a private collection belonging to a retired faculty member named Beaumont who had died in 1989. He did not know what had happened to Beaumont's papers.

Then he said: *Please don't go to that building at night.*

He hung up before Jeppe could ask how he knew about the building.

## End of Session

The agents drove to W. Southern Avenue as the sun dropped behind the South Mountain range. They parked on the shoulder and continued on foot down the dirt access road. The building was visible ahead, pale against the darkening scrub. The door was blue.

Session ended.
